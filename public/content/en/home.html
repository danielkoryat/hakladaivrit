Touch typing: typing fast without looking at the keyboard

Touch typing, also called ten-finger typing, means typing without looking at the keyboard. Each finger controls a fixed set of keys, and over time you build muscle memory that finds each letter automatically.

Whether you are a student, programmer, writer or office worker, mastering touch typing significantly improves your speed and accuracy.

Why learn touch typing?

  • Type faster and more accurately
  • Keep your eyes on the content, not the keys
  • Work more efficiently with less fatigue

No searching for keys, no moving your gaze between the screen and keyboard. Just smooth, fast and accurate typing.

Main benefits

Speed

With regular practice you can reach 200 to 400 characters per minute or more, and finish tasks much faster.

Accuracy

When your fingers know where the keys are, you make fewer mistakes and spend less time correcting them.

Ergonomics

Proper posture and a stable gaze at the screen reduce strain on your neck, back and eyes.

Home row: a s d f / j k l ;

Your fingers rest on the home row at all times. On the left hand: pinky on a, ring on s, middle on d, and index on f. On the right hand: index on j, middle on k, ring on l, and pinky on ;. The keys F and J have a small bump on them, so you can find the home row by touch. Your thumbs rest on the space bar. Every key and every finger is explained in the complete touch typing guide.

Virtual hands in real time

While you type, a keyboard appears below the text with virtual hands: the finger you need to press next lights up and moves toward the key, so you always know which finger is working, without looking at your real hands.

Online typing test

  • Time mode: type as much as you can in 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds.
  • Word mode: type a set number of words: 10, 25, 50 or 100.
  • Quote mode: type a famous quote, with full punctuation.

At the end you see your words per minute (WPM), characters per minute (CPM), accuracy percentage and a list of the keys where you made mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to learn touch typing?

Most people learn all the keys after a week or two of 15-20 minutes of practice per day. Speed continues to improve over time.

How fast is a good typing speed?

Average typing speed is around 35-40 words per minute. 60 and up is considered fast, and 80 and up is excellent. At first it matters more to focus on accuracy, and speed will come.

Do I need an account?

No. You can practice as a guest and your data will be saved in your browser. With an account, results are stored on the server and you can pick up where you left off on any device.

Is the site free?

Yes. All lessons, tests and practice on the site are completely free, with no time limits.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes. The site works for all ages. Lessons start from scratch, the balloon game teaches letter positions in a fun way, and there are short and easy texts about animals and space.

Can I practice on my own material?

Yes. On the your own text page you can paste a summary or article, and the site breaks it into short sections for practice. This way you learn the material and improve your typing at the same time. Accents and special characters are automatically removed, and texts are saved only in your browser.

How do I start?

Start with lesson one, which teaches the index and middle fingers on the home row: f j d k. After each lesson move on to the next, and check your progress with a typing test every few days.
