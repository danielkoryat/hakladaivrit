Why type real texts?

After lessons, the best way to improve is typing full texts: complete sentences, punctuation and words that do not repeat. The library has short texts about history, science, nature and language, at three levels of difficulty. Texts marked "kids" are shorter and easier.

Practice on your own material

On the your own text page you can paste a summary, article or study material, and the site breaks it into short sections for practice. This way you review the material and improve your typing at the same time. Accents and special characters are removed automatically, and texts are saved only in your browser.
