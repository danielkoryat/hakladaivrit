Smart typing practice

Smart practice learns from your mistakes. Every keystroke is recorded: accuracy and how long it takes you to find each key, and practice sessions are built from words containing exactly the letters that are hard for you.

Practice modes

  • Weak keys: Words that contain the letters you get wrong the most often.
  • Letter unlock: Start with six of the most common letters and unlock a new letter only when all the unlocked letters reach the target speed (20-40 words per minute) and 95% accuracy. Each round focuses on the letter furthest from its target, and rounds continue in sequence. Your progress is saved in your browser.
  • Common words: The most frequent words in English, so they become one smooth motion.
  • Sentences: Full sentences with spaces and punctuation, just like real typing.

Error-free mode

In this mode each practice round continues until you type it perfectly without any mistakes. Words you got wrong come back in the next round, until they come out clean.

Error map

Below the practice there is a keyboard colored by your accuracy on each key: the more red a key is, the more you make mistakes on it. With a Google account your data is saved, and on the analysis page custom lessons are built for each weakness.
