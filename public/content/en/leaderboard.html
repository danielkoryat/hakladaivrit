How to get on the leaderboard?

  • Speed: Your best result on the typing test, in a test of at least 10 words and 90% accuracy or better.
  • Balloon game: Your highest score on balloon pop in the last week.
  • XP points: Every test, lesson, practice session, text and game add points. Points level you up: from chick, through turtle, rabbit, fox and cheetah, all the way to rocket.

To appear on the leaderboard, sign in with Google. Only your first name and the first letter of your last name are shown, and you can choose a nickname or hide yourself from the leaderboard at any time.
