Typing speed test

A free typing speed test. Start typing and the timer begins with your first keystroke. At the end you see your words per minute, characters per minute, accuracy percentage and a list of the keys where you made mistakes.

Test modes

  • Time mode: Type as much as you can in 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds.
  • Word mode: Type 10, 25, 50 or 100 words and see how long it takes.
  • Quote mode: Type a famous English quote, with full punctuation.

You can add punctuation and numbers to make the test harder, and show virtual hands with the keyboard that display which finger presses which key.

How is speed calculated?

A word in the test is 5 characters including spaces. If you type 200 correct characters per minute, your speed is 40 words per minute. Accuracy is the percentage of correct keystrokes out of all keystrokes. Average typing speed is 35-40 words per minute, and 60 and up is considered fast.
