Typing game for kids: Balloon pop

Balloon pop is a free typing game that teaches kids where each letter is on the keyboard. Balloons with letters float up from the bottom, and children type the letter to pop the balloon before it floats away. A keyboard with virtual hands below the game shows which finger to use.

Three levels

  • Home row: Just the eight home row letters: a s d f j k l ;. Good for age 6 and up and for beginners.
  • All letters: All letters of the alphabet, including the rare ones.
  • Words: Short words. You type the word letter by letter, and the balloon pops when the word is complete.

How to earn points?

Each popped balloon gives you points, and a streak of balloons without mistakes doubles them. Every 10 balloons the game levels up and balloons move faster. A balloon that escapes takes one heart, and after three hearts the game is over. High scores appear on the leaderboard.
